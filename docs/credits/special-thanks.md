# Special Thanks

Verse-atile is being built with gratitude toward the creators, maintainers, educators, testers, artists, researchers, open-source developers, standards bodies, tool vendors, and community members whose work helps make modern Unreal Engine development possible.

## Rights-truthful credit rule

This page must never guess or invent attribution.

A person, project, repository, asset creator, company, or community is added only when the public credit is supported by a verified source, license, contribution record, purchase/acquisition record, or direct authorization.

Credits do **not** imply endorsement, partnership, sponsorship, ownership transfer, or that a third party authored Verse-atile unless that is explicitly true.

## Credit categories

As public-release evidence is verified, this page may include:

- Open-source and permissively licensed software authors
- CC0 / Creative Commons asset creators
- Documentation and educational resources
- Testers and community contributors
- Public standards and technology foundations
- Publicly credited Unreal ecosystem creators
- Providers and services used through optional adapters
- People who directly contribute approved public work to Verse-atile

## Never-drop credit rule

If a verified third-party contribution, permissive library, public-domain work, Creative Commons asset, or required attribution is used in a distributed Verse-atile release, its applicable credit/license notice must remain preserved even if the internal implementation later changes.

Detailed third-party license notices belong on the **Third-Party Notices** page and, where required, in shipped product notices.
